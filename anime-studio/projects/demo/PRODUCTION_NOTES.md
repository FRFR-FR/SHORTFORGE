# NEON VEIL — Run the Last Light

This is the assistant-authored ANIMEFORGE showcase sequence. Its character, environments, drone choreography, lighting, camera and animation code are authored directly into the repository. It does not use Claude API calls.

## Shot plan (15 seconds / 30 fps / 450 frames)

1. Rain-soaked avenue sprint — low tracking view, animated limb cycle, drifting scarf and street parallax.
2. Sentinel-09 enters — rotating scan rings, pulsing optic, magenta search cone and camera tilt.
3. Hard skid — body recoil, planted-leg pose, arm reach and short impact shake.
4. Photon blade ignition — cyan emissive sword, reflected rim lighting and camera push-in.
5. Aerial counterattack — jump arc, animated joints, trailing scarf, speed lines and rising dolly.
6. Slash finish — curved energy streak, brief impact shake and composed final hero hold.

## Original art pack

- 12 SVG character layers: head, torso, upper/lower arms, upper/lower legs, scarf and blade.
- 3 environment plates: distant skyline, midground towers/street and foreground framing.
- Hand-authored Three.js Sentinel-09 drone, escort drone, speeder, blade, scan cone, slash geometry, rain and ember particles.
- The layered cutout rig uses parent-child joints with deterministic time-based animation.

The first CI render is a 640×360 smoke profile. Use the 1280×720 preview profile for review and 1920×1080 master only after an art review. Technical QA does not establish studio-level anatomy or animation polish by itself.
