## Dramatic premise

Project Glasswing may have changed the defensive cybersecurity landscape by giving selected defenders access to Claude Mythos Preview. But has it “saved the internet,” or has it merely made discovery faster while leaving the hard work of verifying, patching and deploying fixes to people?

## Distinct positions

- **Liam — host/evidence examiner:** frames the story, challenges the headline, and reaches a measured verdict.
- **George — open-source maintainer:** argues that vulnerability discovery is only the opening move; maintainers need time, funding, testing and deployment capacity. He also stresses dual-use risk.
- **Eponine — critical-infrastructure defender:** argues that earlier discovery and coordinated access can materially reduce exposure in systems whose failure affects millions.
