# Seven specialist review passes

Run these as separate review passes in ChatGPT or with human reviewers. Each pass must return **Pass / Needs revision / Blocked**, list timestamp/frame references, and give actionable corrections. Do not merge the seven roles into one vague “looks good” response.

## 1. Story editor — hook, clarity and payoff

Check whether the first second communicates a compelling premise; the protagonist's goal and obstacle are understandable without explanation; every beat changes the situation; the ending delivers a clear payoff. Flag filler, exposition and confusing cause/effect.

## 2. Director of photography — framing and camera language

Review 9:16 safe composition, subject scale on a phone, headroom, eyelines, foreground/midground/background separation, motivated camera movement and continuity across cuts. Flag cropped faces, hidden actions, accidental camera shake and shots that do not support the beat.

## 3. Character animator — anatomy, weight and performance

Inspect eyes, mouth, limbs, pivots, silhouette, planted feet, weight shifts, anticipation, follow-through, contact points and emotional performance. Flag missing/duplicated/misplaced features, foot sliding, joint popping, teleporting, floating, collision and implausible timing.

## 4. Environment and continuity supervisor

Check that each setting is purpose-built for the action, props remain consistent, character scale is stable, shadows/contact points agree, objects do not pop in/out, and all movement has a clear path. Flag generic or mismatched backgrounds, intersections and unexplained spatial changes.

## 5. Art director — style bible and visual quality

Compare every shot to the project's style bible: palette, material response, lighting, detail density, silhouette language and visual hierarchy. Flag style drift, flat lighting, noisy materials, inconsistent scale, unfinished assets and unclear focal points.

## 6. Dialogue and sound editor

Check narration timing, pronunciation, pacing, intelligibility, clipping, silence and dialogue-to-action alignment. Confirm no background music has been added unless requested. Flag unnecessary sound, missing motivated effects, abrupt audio cuts and narration that describes what is already obvious.

## 7. Technical QA and rights reviewer

Verify 30 fps, exact aspect ratio, expected duration, clean frame edges, no blank/frozen frames, valid H.264/AAC streams, no missing textures, and correct audio. Confirm every external asset is owned, public-domain or properly licensed and no reference creator's protected material has been copied.

## Required report format

```text
Role: [review pass]
Status: PASS | NEEDS REVISION | BLOCKED
Timestamp/frame references:
Findings:
Required fixes:
Recheck result:
```

A production is ready only when all seven roles pass and a human has visually inspected the contact sheet and any flagged time ranges. Pixel heuristics cannot prove good storytelling or character anatomy.
