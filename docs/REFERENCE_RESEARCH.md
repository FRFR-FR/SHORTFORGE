# Ten-reference short-form research worksheet

Use this worksheet before designing a new format. The purpose is to identify broad, reusable production patterns, not to recreate another creator's work.

## Sampling method

Default to a rolling three-year publication window. Choose ten publicly viewable shorts in the same broad niche, prioritizing examples with strong visible engagement and a publish date within that window. Record the date and view count as a dated snapshot; these change. Include varied creators so the result is not a single-channel imitation study.

For every reference, record:

1. URL, creator, title, publication date and observed view count/date.
2. First-second hook type (question, visual surprise, premise, action, reveal).
3. Approximate shot duration and frequency of visual changes.
4. Camera language (locked, dolly, pan, push-in, orbit, handheld imitation).
5. Character staging and readability on a phone screen.
6. Use of captions, graphic overlays and negative space.
7. Broad sound categories (dialogue, ambience, impacts, transitions); do not reuse protected audio.
8. Story structure (setup, escalation, payoff) at a high level.
9. What appears to hold attention and what feels weak.
10. One abstract technique worth testing in an original way.

## Comparison matrix

After ten entries, summarize only aggregate observations: recurring hook families, common rhythm ranges, transition density, framing preferences, caption placement, and typical payoff timing. Note outliers and uncertainty. Do not transcribe dialogue or create a shot-by-shot imitation map.

## Rights and originality boundary

Do not download, scrape or reuse reference videos unless you have the rights and the platform terms permit it. Do not reproduce scripts, dialogue, characters, artwork, signature props, exact shot order, distinctive scene compositions, channel branding or audio. Create a new premise, new scene sequence, original characters, original environments and independently authored dialogue. Obtain proper licenses for any stock footage or external asset.

## Project decision

The production brief should state:

- Three broad patterns being adapted (in abstract terms).
- Three patterns intentionally avoided.
- What is distinct about the new story and style.
- Which metrics will be used to judge the experiment (retention curve, completion rate, replay, comments, or user testing).
